Legacy `parse` and `parse_with_limits` retain their behavior. Recovery records real BLOCK, ERROR_NODE and INCOMPLETE CST nodes, bounded declaration/site inventories and actual receiver-token spans. Missing-token sites are zero-width. Statement anchors retain later statements; complete top-level siblings survive damaged function bodies. Unknown headers, lexical tails, macros and ambiguous unclosed non-function bodies remain explicit opaque contexts.

`parse_complete_items(&RecoverySyntax, &[usize], Limits)` accepts sorted unique complete declaration indices and reparses the actual original full-source token ranges with absolute spans. Failed productions and internal placeholders never escape. Impl items lack a source span in the existing AST and are unsupported by this helper. The crate-private sealed body-context capture and body-fragment parser support the separate incremental lane, using fresh real-source lexing and unchanged prefix/header/suffix checks; no wrappers or padded source are constructed.

The recovery driver precharges parsing and carries the remaining allowance into model assembly.
