Sandbox tokens carry guest-supplied scopes, spans and optional numeric DefIds; that transport alone does not prove hygiene, trusted origin or macro confinement. Implement:

5. An authority checker over a versioned resolved-operation/call/handler graph. Unresolved/unknown graph edges fail closed.
6. Every generated operation must fit BOTH the macro's declared emits policy and the caller's ceiling, including injected dependency calls, handler construction and transitive callees. Do not certify arbitrary token text by trusting a guest-provided summary of what it does; accepted authority evidence is conditional on trusted resolved graph input.
